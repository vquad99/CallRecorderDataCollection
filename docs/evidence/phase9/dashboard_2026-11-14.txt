How the figures were checked (DASH-003)
---------------------------------------
backend/tests/test_dashboard_filters.py builds one project with three finished recordings whose
lengths and moments are set in the database:

  phone call, Hindi, moderator "mod" ........... 2.0 h, started 23:55 India time on day A
  face-to-face, Hindi, "mod" ................... 1.5 h, started 00:05 India time on day A+1
  face-to-face, Tamil (dialect Madurai), "mod2",
  task approved ................................ 0.5 h, started 11:30 India time on day A+2

The first two are ten minutes apart: neighbouring days in India, the same day in UTC (the test
checks that too). test_every_figure_matches_sql_written_on_its_own compares the dashboard's
answer with SQL written separately in the test for: hours, approved hours and recordings by
mode; hours per day (the day taken in Asia/Kolkata); tasks by status; output per moderator;
storage in use; phone calls placed and recorded.

Filters (DASH-002): test_filters_narrow_every_figure (mode, language, dialect, status, staff,
several together, storage follows, the lists offered, wrong values refused);
test_chosen_days_count_in_india_time_and_are_bounded (one day, the next day, both, empty days,
only a first or only a last day, no chosen days unchanged, first after last 422, more than 366
days 422, exactly 366 accepted); test_filters_respect_who_is_asking (a moderator within their
own work and never by person, a client viewer without people, another project's admin 404).

In a real browser: frontend/journeys/dashboard.spec.ts, the administrator step: Recording type
narrows the answer (telephony recordings 0, calls placed 0, the staff list present); Chosen days
with two days gives exactly two days in the chart; days the wrong way round are caught on the
page; Clear filters takes everything off.
Page tests: frontend/src/features/dashboard/dashboard.test.tsx (each filter is sent; chosen
days; the phone-call card; a client viewer has no status or staff filter).

What the tests show (backend/tests/test_operation_alerts.py)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Nothing wrong: nothing kept, no email.
- An export fails: both alert addresses get one email, "1 thing needs attention", with the
  sentence, since when, the address of the status page and where the runbook is.
- The same thing still wrong, and more of it: no second email; the count on record follows.
- Something else goes wrong (storage does not answer): an email at once naming both.
- Still wrong 25 hours after the last email: one reminder, not two.
- The Super Admin's status page (GET /system/status) lists the same, with how many addresses
  are told.
- Everything put right: one "All clear" email, then quiet; the rows stay, marked resolved.
- No alert address: no email, the status page still lists what is wrong.
- Another process holds the review (a database lock): this one leaves everything alone.
- The mail server is down: the problem is kept and nobody counts as told; the email goes out
  with the next look once mail works.
- What it looks for in recordings and calls: a failed recording, an upload that waited 3 hours,
  a call waiting for a person are found; an upload only just made is not "stuck".
